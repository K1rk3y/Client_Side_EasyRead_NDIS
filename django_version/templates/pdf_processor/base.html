Welcome to Intelife Easy Read Tools

Intelife is dedicated to supporting people with disabilities to live more independently and confidently. Our Easy Read Tools are designed to help you convert complex PDF and DOCX documents into easy-to-understand formats, making information more accessible for everyone.

  • Purpose: Simplify documents so they are easier to read and understand.
  • How to use: Upload your PDF or DOCX file, and our tool will process and present the content in an easy read format.
  • Guidance: Use the Simple mode for basic conversion, or switch to Advance mode for more customization and features.

If you need help, please contact the Intelife team or refer to our help section.
