Easy Read Generator™

Transforming documentation into Easy Read Content

The Easy Read Generator™ is a breakthrough AI-powered tool designed to automate the transformation of documentation into Easy Read formats—making essential information accessible to everyone, especially individuals with low literacy levels or cognitive disabilities.

Developed in collaboration with the McCusker Centre for Citizenship and refined through real-world internships and stakeholder feedback, this tool empowers organizations to create inclusive content without the traditional barriers of cost, time, and specialised training.

Who Will Benefit Most?

  • Business Owners: Ensure your communications are inclusive and compliant with accessibility standards, enhancing brand reputation and stakeholder trust.
  • Human Resources Professionals: Simplify policy documents, onboarding materials, and internal communications to support diverse employee needs.
  • Learning & Development Advisors: Create training content that is universally understandable, improving engagement and retention across all learner profiles.

Key Use Cases

  • Converting HR policies, safety protocols, and training guides into Easy Read formats.
  • Enhancing accessibility for NDIS participants and other service recipients.
  • Supporting inclusive hiring and onboarding practices.
  • Promoting digital inclusion in the not-for-profit and public sectors.

Core Benefits

  • Accessibility at Scale: Automatically generate Easy Read versions of complex documents, reducing reliance on external consultants.
  • Efficiency & Cost Savings: Cut down the time and expense traditionally required to produce accessible content.
  • AI-Driven Quality: Leverages advanced techniques like Retrieval Augmented Generation and fine-tuned LLMs to ensure clarity and relevance.
  • Social Impact: Promotes inclusion and equity by increasing the availability of Easy Read content online.
  • Workforce Development: Offers students and interns exposure to real-world AI applications in the not-for-profit sector.

Vision for the Future

The ultimate goal is to launch a self-service Easy Read Generator accessible to all, enabling organisations of any size to embrace inclusive communication practices.
