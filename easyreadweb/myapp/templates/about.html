About Us

We believe in a world where every individual—regardless of ability—can live a life of purpose, dignity, and independence. As a leading employer of people living with a disability based in Western Australia, we are dedicated to empowering people with intellectual disabilities through meaningful employment, social connection, and community inclusion.

Our mission is to enable independence by creating inclusive pathways to work and life. We do this not only through our supported employment programs and commercial services, but also by embracing the transformative power of technology.

As part of our commitment to innovation, Intelife is actively investing in AI and digital technologies to enhance the lives of the people we support. From intelligent systems that streamline service delivery to digital tools that foster autonomy and communication, we are exploring how technology can break down barriers and open new possibilities. Our Technology & Digital Innovation team is focused on solutions that are ethical, inclusive, and co-designed with the people who use them.

We are proud to be part of a movement that sees technology not as a replacement for human care, but as a powerful enabler of human potential. The Easy Read Generator is the first of a portfolio of solutions supporting this movement.
