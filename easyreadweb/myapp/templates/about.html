About Us

Easy Read Intelife committed to making information accessible and easy to understand for everyone. Originally developed to support people with disabilities, our platform now also helps a broader community—including new employees, older adults, and others—by transforming complex documents into easy read formats. Whether it’s onboarding materials or bank statements, we help make information clear, inclusive, and usable.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, accessible solutions that enable people of all abilities to access, understand, and use information in their daily lives.

Who We Are

We are a team of passionate professionals, support workers, and technologists, co-developing with the McCusker Centre for Citizenship and in partnership with Intelife. Our goal is to break down barriers to information, enabling clarity and confidence for people from all walks of life.

Our Values

  • Accessibility
  • Inclusion
  • Empowerment
  • Innovation
  • Collaboration
