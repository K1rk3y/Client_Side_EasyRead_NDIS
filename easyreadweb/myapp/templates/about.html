About Us

Easy Read Intelife is dedicated to empowering people with disabilities by making information accessible and easy to understand. Our platform transforms complex documents into easy read formats, supporting independence and inclusion for all.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, accessible solutions that enable people with disabilities to access, understand, and use information in their daily lives.

Who We Are

We are a team of passionate professionals, support workers, and technologists, co-developing with the Mccusker Centre for Citizenship and in partnership with Intelife. Our goal is to break down barriers to information for people of all abilities.

Our Values

  • Accessibility
  • Inclusion
  • Empowerment
  • Innovation
  • Collaboration
