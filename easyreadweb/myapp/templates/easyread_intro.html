What is Easy Read?

Easy Read is a style of presenting information that uses simple words, short sentences, and supportive images to make content easier to understand. It is designed for people with low literacy, cognitive disabilities, or anyone who benefits from clear, straightforward communication.

Example of Easy Read documents


Key Features of Easy Read

  • Short, simple sentences
  • Everyday words and no jargon
  • Large, clear font and plenty of white space
  • Supportive images or icons for each idea
  • Logical order and clear structure

Who Benefits from Easy Read?

  • People with intellectual or cognitive disabilities
  • People with low literacy or English as a second language
  • Older adults
  • Anyone who prefers clear, simple information

Why Use Easy Read?

  • Promotes inclusion and independence
  • Improves understanding and reduces confusion
  • Meets accessibility and legal requirements
  • Helps organizations communicate with a wider audience
